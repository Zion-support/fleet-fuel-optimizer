# Use Cases — Fleet Fuel Optimizer

[Live app](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/) · [Plans](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/plans/) · [Discovery call](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/)

## 1. Regional delivery fleet
A 60-van delivery fleet saves 11% on diesel in 90 days through fuel-stop optimization and idle-time coaching.

## 2. Long-haul trucking
Cheapest-corridor fueling plans per route cut cost per mile; out-of-route miles drop 22% after driver scorecards launch.

## 3. Field-service vehicles
Technician vans benchmarked per depot; worst-quartile driving behavior addressed via [Technician Coach AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/technician-coach-ai/).

## 4. Fuel fraud detection
Fuel-card transactions matched against GPS location; purchases away from the vehicle are flagged and reconciled in [Invoice Match AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/invoice-match-ai/).

## 5. Sustainability reporting
Verified CO₂ reductions feed ESG reports with month-over-month evidence.
