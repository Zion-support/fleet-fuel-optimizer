## What it does

Fleet Fuel Optimizer analyzes telematics, routes, loads, and fuel prices to recommend the cheapest fueling stops, the most efficient routes, and the driving behaviors that burn the most diesel — typically saving 8–15% on fuel.

## Key features

- **Fuel-stop optimization** — cheapest stations along each route, updated daily.
- **Route efficiency scoring** — detours, idle time, and out-of-route miles quantified.
- **Driver behavior insights** — harsh acceleration, speeding, and idling per driver.
- **Fleet benchmarks** — compare vehicles, depots, and regions on L/100km or MPG.
- **Savings tracker** — verified savings report month over month.

## Who it's for

Logistics operators, delivery fleets, and field-service companies with 10+ vehicles.
